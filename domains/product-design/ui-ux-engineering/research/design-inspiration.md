# Design Inspiration & Micro-Interaction Research

## Purpose
This guide covers how to gather visual design inspiration, micro-interaction benchmarks, and motion choreography without copying generic AI aesthetics.

---

## 1. Aesthetic Foundations vs. AI Clichés

| Generic AI Cliché | Intentional Product Craftsmanship |
| :--- | :--- |
| Over-saturated purple/cyan glow gradients everywhere. | Restrained, purpose-driven brand accents (e.g., slate-900 with crisp indigo-600 focus points). |
| Uniform rounded-3xl borders on all cards causing visual mush. | Purposeful corner radii: `rounded-lg` (8px) for inputs/buttons, `rounded-2xl` (16px) for surface cards. |
| Centered floating text with zero typographic hierarchy. | Crisp typographic rhythm with structured line heights (`leading-snug`, `tracking-tight`). |
| Unfunctional animated gradient borders. | Meaningful micro-animations: 150ms hover scale, 200ms accordion ease-out, smooth backdrop blur. |
