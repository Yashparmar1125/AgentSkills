# Usability Testing & Heuristic Evaluation Guide

## 1. Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics
1. **Visibility of System Status**: Real-time loading indicators, upload progress bars, auto-save status.
2. **Match Between System and Real World**: Use terms students and teachers understand (not database keys).
3. **User Control and Freedom**: Clear "Cancel" and "Undo" actions, modal dismiss on `Esc`.
4. **Consistency and Standards**: Identical button variants and status colors across every page.
5. **Error Prevention**: Confirmation modals on destructive deletions, input format masks.
6. **Recognition Rather Than Recall**: Visible options, contextual dropdown filters.
7. **Flexibility and Efficiency of Use**: Keyboard shortcuts for power users, search filters.
8. **Aesthetic and Minimalist Design**: High signal-to-noise ratio; remove visual clutter.
9. **Help Users Recognize and Recover from Errors**: Plain-language error messages with fix suggestions.
10. **Help and Documentation**: Contextual help tooltips and onboarding walkthroughs.
