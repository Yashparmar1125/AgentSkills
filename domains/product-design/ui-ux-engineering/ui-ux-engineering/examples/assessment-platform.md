# Case Study: Examination & Assessment Platform (AGCL Exams)

## Context
High-stakes academic examination platform with live proctoring, mathematical score explainability, and multi-tenant reporting.

## Key UX Deliverables Implemented
1. **Focus-Preserving Exam Hall**: Distraction-free question viewer, sticky countdown timer, and automated answer persistence.
2. **Recorded Keystrokes & Telemetry**: Formatted `<kbd>` badges and second-level incident timeline.
3. **Proctoring Score Explainability**: Accordion breakdown revealing the exact deduction formula.
4. **Zero-Waterfall Reports**: Concurrent TanStack Query hooks with 12 matching animated skeletons.
5. **High-Resolution Lightbox**: Portal modal with zoom, pan, rotate, and full-resolution inspection.
