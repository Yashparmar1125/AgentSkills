# Competitor & Market UX Analysis Guide

## Purpose
This guide outlines how to benchmark competitor interfaces and translate market standards into superior product experiences.

---

## 1. Competitor Evaluation Matrix

| Competitor | Core User Flow | Strengths | Weaknesses / Friction | Opportunity for Our Product |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Industry Leader A** | 3-step wizard | Minimalist, clean visual rhythm | Obscures summary metrics; no undo | Add floating preview bar with instant undo |
| **Direct Competitor B** | Monolithic table | High data density | Overwhelming cognitive load on mobile | Transform table into card stacks on screens < 640px |
| **Enterprise SaaS C** | Modal-heavy flow | Granular permissions | Deeply nested modals cause disorientation | Use persistent slide-over drawers with breadcrumbs |

---

## 2. Competitive Heuristics
- **Fitts' Law**: Ensure primary CTAs are placed in high-efficiency click zones.
- **Hick's Law**: Reduce decision time by categorizing and progressively disclosing advanced options.
- **Jakob's Law**: Users spend most time on other sites; maintain established mental models for search, navigation, and auth.
